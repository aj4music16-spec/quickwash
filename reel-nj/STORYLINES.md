# Storylines — QuickWash, New Jersey app-download reel

Three different films were considered. **A was built** (the brief asked for the finished reel, no stops).

## A · "Full Service" — built
*Type carries the argument, as a drive through one world.* New Jersey is the only state where you
don't pump your own gas. The film is a drive past Jersey roadside signs: a gas-station sign makes
the brag, an old black-and-white billboard asks why you're still washing your own car, and a
squeegee wipes the old way off the screen.
- First two seconds: a "FULL SERVICE" gas pylon reading JERSEY DOESN'T PUMP GAS; "GAS" gets painted orange on beat 2; whip-pan.
- Beats: hook sign → old billboard → squeegee to colour → the app books in under 20 s → NJ map lights 13 counties → price sign → end card.
- Look: bright sky, painted steel signs, navy and white with orange as the one accent; real photos live on the billboard; the real app on a phone.
- Sound: a Jersey-club-style beat at 144 BPM, tinny "old radio" under the black-and-white stretch, the drop on the squeegee.

## B · "Salt Season" — not built
*A moment in someone's day.* A grey Jersey winter morning; a commuter looks at a salt-crusted car
and goes back inside. The car gets cleaned in place while the frame stays put.
- First two seconds: close on road salt crusted on a door sill; a finger writes WASH ME.
- Beats: salt close-up → "it's 28° out" → phone books it → detailer works while the kettle boils → clean car, same shot.
- Look: photographic, cold blue to warm; one locked frame that changes. Needs new winter photos.
- Sound: winter quiet, a single piano figure, the beat only when the car is done.
- Best run December to March.

## C · "What Exit?" — not built (the bold one)
*A question with the input.* "What exit?" is how Jersey asks where you're from. A Parkway-style exit
list scrolls and every exit lights up as "QuickWash comes here".
- First two seconds: a green highway sign reading WHAT EXIT? fills the frame.
- Beats: WHAT EXIT? → exits tick past (Clifton, Paramus, Red Bank…) → every one lights → ALL OF THEM → app → end card.
- Look: only highway-sign green, white and reflective yellow.
- Sound: cut on the beat, a turn-signal click as the metronome.
- Risk: it sells coverage more than the service itself.
