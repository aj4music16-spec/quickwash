DetailerOS: what is live vs proposed

Only advertise the left column. The right column is ready for "coming soon" or launch ads once it ships.

Live (screen recordings, App Store, pitch)

  • Calendar with day / week / month and job cards
  • Create booking for your own client: packages, extras, upsell chips, tap-to-pay or invoice link, SMS / email notify
  • Client list with send offer / reschedule / cancel
  • Public storefront with your prices and a book-now link (Apple Pay, card, Klarna, Affirm, Cash App)
  • QuickWash jobs sent to your area, accept or decline
  • Payouts and earnings, tips, withdraw to bank
  • On-my-way / arrived notifications, routing, ratings
  • Detailer code so clients rebook you
  • Supply Center (Detailing World store) in the older app; confirm it is still in DetailerOS

Proposed (when shipped)

  • Home dashboard: paid / unpaid cards, own-client vs QuickWash-client markers, SMS payment reminder
  • Client Rolodex: sorted by time since last wash, VIP tags, wash history
  • Rewards: "1 job away from $50" challenges, leaderboards
  • Business Code bookings for clubs and offices
  • Deposits and invoices from sales reps
  • Crew / employee management
  • Recurring holds and time-block redesign
