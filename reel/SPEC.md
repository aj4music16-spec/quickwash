# QuickWash — "Your Week, Reset." (vertical reel)

1080×1920, 30 fps, 16.6 s. Social vertical × commercial: hook ≤1 s, visible change by 3 s,
change again at ~6 s, payoff before 15 s, end card held ≥2 s (CTA on screen 2.2 s).

**Claim:** your car collects your week; QuickWash resets it without you leaving home.
**Proof shown:** clutter piles up → foam, brush, shine → detailer in your driveway → you, relaxed.

## Rules

- Text-led, legible muted. Every word inside 168–1330 px vertically, nothing under the right
  rail (x > 930). Titles Inter 900, 112–150 px; one accent word in orange (#ff6a13).
- Frame 0 = the hook line already on screen over the crumbs (it is the thumbnail).
- Photos are 16:9 plates reframed vertically: a 3413×1920 image panned to the subject's x.
  Camera moves on the wrapper only; titles land and hold dead still.
- Transitions (4 kinds): hard cuts on the beat (montage), whip-down into the reset, blur swap
  between the reset words, colour flood into the end card.
- Pace: montage 0.5 s cuts (120 BPM grid); story beats 1.1–1.4 s; end card 2.2 s.
